Delete your Mix & Reveal account

Mix & Reveal: Color Puzzle is published by TonTech. You can delete your game account and its data at any time.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Mix & Reveal.
  2. Tap Settings, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm twice.

Your account is deleted from our server straight away.

Without the game

Email info@tontech.co with the subject "Mix & Reveal account deletion" and your friend code (it looks like MIX-7K2Q and is shown in Settings and on the Friends screen). We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account, username, friend code and sign-in links.
  • Your profile, statistics, Duel rating, Tag Team data, coins, boosters and items.
  • Friends, blocks, challenges, invites and invite rewards.
  • Your leaderboard entries, ghost runs, push token and notifications.
  • Gameplay statistics linked to your account, and those sent from the same install before you signed in.

What is kept

  • Match records, for up to 12 months, with an account identifier that no longer leads to any profile.
  • Reports about player behaviour, for up to 12 months after they were last updated.
  • Purchase verification records without your account identifier, to stop a purchase being used twice and for accounting.

Purchases you made stay with your Apple or Google account; deleting your game account does not refund them. Solo progress on your device is removed when you uninstall the game. More details: Privacy Policy.
