Mix & Reveal: Color Puzzle

Mix colors, pour paint, reveal the picture

App Store: coming soonGoogle Play: coming soon

iPhone and Android.

Every level is a blank sketch and a palette of paint cups. Pour the right colors into the right places and a picture appears, one patch at a time.

Mix, pour, reveal

  • Pick paint buckets off the conveyor and pour them onto the matching patches of the sketch.
  • Mix two colors in a cup to make a new one: red and yellow make orange, blue and yellow make green.
  • Plan your slots: the wrong mix makes mud. Paint every patch and the artwork comes alive.

Play with friends

  • Duel: race a friend on the same picture, live. Their canvas shows as a mini-map, and three clean pours in a row charge a Splat.
  • Tag Team: two players share one canvas. One holds the brush, the other mixes, and TAG swaps the roles.
  • Party Race: up to 8 friends, three quick rounds and a podium at the end.
  • Challenge a friend to the same picture and race their ghost.
  • Invite friends with your personal link. When they reach level 10 you get coins and the Studio Friend frame.
  • No chat, just friendly emotes.

Calm by design

  • Short levels, no timers in solo play.
  • Play offline, anywhere.
  • A gallery that fills up with every picture you reveal.
