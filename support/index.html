Email info@tontech.co. It helps to include your device model, OS version, your friend code (Settings or the Friends screen) and a short description of what happened.

Frequently asked questions

How do invites work?

Open Play with friends → Invite friends (or Settings → Invite a friend) and share your personal link. When your friend installs the game from it, you become friends and they get 200 coins. When they reach level 10 you get 300 coins and the Studio Friend frame, for up to 10 friends.

I installed from an invite link but nothing happened.

On iPhone the game asks "Were you invited by...?" after the tutorial, if you opened the link in the last 2 hours. On Android the invite comes with the Google Play install. If neither worked, add your friend with their friend code (MIX-XXXX) on the Friends screen. You are still friends; only the welcome coins need the invite link.

My friend's invite link says it doesn't look right.

Friend codes look like MIX-7K2Q: four letters and digits after MIX-, without I, O, 0 and 1. Check the link, or type the code in the game.

Does the invite page read my clipboard?

No, never. See the Privacy Policy for what an invite link does.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the game and go to Settings → Delete account, then confirm twice. This removes your profile, friend code, friendships, challenges and invite records from our server. Your solo progress stays on your device until you uninstall.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Solo levels, the gallery and practice work offline. Friends, challenges and invites need a connection.

How do I report or block another player?

Open Play with friends, find the player and choose Report or Block. There is no text chat in the game; players only use emotes.

Response time

We aim to respond within a couple of business days.
