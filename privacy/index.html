Mix & Reveal Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-17 (draft, to be finalised with the App Privacy and Data safety answers before launch)

This policy explains what information Mix & Reveal: Color Puzzle (the "game", operated by TonTech) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. By using the game you agree to this policy.

1. The short version

  • You can play without an email, phone number or real name. Solo play works offline.
  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone audio.
  • The game shows ads. If you allow tracking, ads can be personalised and your advertising ID is used for install attribution.
  • Invite links never use your clipboard. An invite link opened on an iPhone is remembered for 2 hours only (section 4).
  • You can delete your game account in the game: Settings → Delete account.

2. Who we are

Mix & Reveal is a color puzzle game published by TonTech. For privacy questions, see section 11.

3. Information we collect

Game account on our server

For friends, challenges and invites, the game creates an account on TonTech's server at mixreveal-api.tontech.co. The account uses a device-generated identifier, not your email or phone number. The server may store:

  • the device-generated account identifier and, if you link one, your Apple, Google or Game Center sign-in;
  • an optional username (moderated; shown to friends), your avatar, frame and game stats;
  • your friend code, your friends list, challenges you send or play, and the invites you send or accept (who invited whom, when, and whether the invite reached level 10);
  • reports and blocks you submit about other players, and reports made about you;
  • push notification tokens, if you allow notifications.

The game has no text chat. Players interact with preset emotes only.

Gameplay analytics

The game sends gameplay events (for example level started or won, purchases, ads shown) with a random per-install identifier to our own server, to balance levels and fix problems. They are not sold or shared for advertising.

Advertising identifier and install attribution

The advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Google advertising ID on Android) is read only if you allow tracking: on iOS after you accept Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. It is used by our ad partner (Google AdMob) and, with a random per-install identifier, sent to NUVIAD (notify.nuviad.com) so we can measure which campaigns led to an install. Apple's SKAdNetwork reports installs without identifiers.

Crash reports

Crash reports contain technical diagnostics (device model, OS version, error details), not your identity.

4. Invite links

When a friend shares an invite or challenge link with you and you open it:

  • The page asks our server for your friend's username and avatar to show who invited you.
  • On an iPhone or iPad only, our server also notes the invite for 2 hours: your friend's code, the time, the word "iOS", and a scrambled (salted hash) form of the first part of your network address (for IPv4 the first three of four numbers, for IPv6 the first three of eight groups). Your full IP address is not stored.
  • If you install the game within those 2 hours, it asks: "Were you invited by {name}?". Only if you answer Yes do you become friends and receive your welcome coins. The note is used once and then deleted, and every note is deleted after 2 hours whether it was used or not.
  • On Android, the invite code travels with the Google Play install (the Play install referrer), so nothing is noted on our server when you open the page.
  • Nothing is read from or written to your clipboard, and invite data is never used for ads or tracking.

5. Ads and purchases

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Before personalised ads, the game asks for consent where the law requires it (Google's consent form) and, on iOS, for tracking permission. In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we receive a receipt to deliver what you bought, never your payment details.

6. How we use information

  • To run the game: show your profile to friends, run challenges and invites, and pay invite rewards.
  • To keep the community safe: handle reports and blocks and moderate usernames.
  • To improve the game: gameplay analytics and crash reports.
  • To fund the game: ads, and measuring which campaigns lead to installs (advertising ID only with your permission).

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Sharing

  • Google AdMob: ads (see Google's privacy policy).
  • NUVIAD: advertising ID and per-install identifier for install attribution, only with your permission.
  • Apple and Google: store services, sign-in if you link it, purchases and SKAdNetwork.
  • Other players: your username, avatar, frame, stats and friend code are visible to players you connect with. Your username and avatar are shown on your invite links.
  • TonTech's server (mixreveal-api.tontech.co) stores the account data in section 3.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Tracking: decline the ATT prompt or change it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Invites: answer No to "Were you invited by...?" and nothing is linked.
  • Delete your account: in the game, open Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile, friend code, friendships, challenges, your invite records and rewards, any pending invite notes for your links, and the account itself.
  • Local data: progress is also stored on your device; uninstalling the game removes it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Contact us to exercise them.

9. Retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Invite notes from iPhone link visits are deleted after 2 hours at the latest. Analytics events are kept for up to 2 years in aggregate form.

10. Children

Mix & Reveal is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact info@tontech.co.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted here with a new effective date.
