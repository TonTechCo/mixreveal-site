Mix & Reveal Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-18 (draft, to be finalised with the App Privacy and Data safety answers before launch)

This policy explains what information Mix & Reveal: Color Puzzle (the "game", published by TonTech) collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and the choices you have.

1. The short version

  • No email, phone number or real name is needed. Solo play works offline.
  • The game does not collect your contacts, photos, microphone audio or precise location.
  • Your game account uses a random identifier that the game creates. Your username is checked by an automated moderation service.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Personalised ads and the advertising identifier are only used with your consent and, on iPhone, your tracking permission.
  • Gameplay statistics go to our own server, not to analytics companies.
  • Invite links never use your clipboard. An invite link opened on an iPhone is remembered for 2 hours only (section 5).
  • You can delete your account in the game: Settings → Delete account (section 9).

2. Who we are

TonTech publishes Mix & Reveal and is responsible for your information. Contact: info@tontech.co (section 13).

3. Information we collect

Your game account

On first launch the game creates an account on TonTech's game server (mixreveal-api.tontech.co, a self-hosted Nakama server operated by TonTech). The account uses a random device identifier created by the game. It is not your email, phone number or the device's advertising identifier. If you choose to link Sign in with Apple, Google or Game Center (where offered), we store the account identifier that service gives us so you can restore your account on a new device. We never receive your password.

The account holds:

  • your username, avatar, frame and game statistics (levels completed, Duel wins and losses, Tag Team wins, Gallery highlights);
  • your friend code, your friends list and the players you block;
  • challenges you send or play (the level, your time, stars, moves and a replay of your moves);
  • invites you send or accept (who invited whom, when, and whether the invite reached level 10, for the invite reward);
  • your coins, boosters and the items you bought (see "Purchases" below);
  • a push notification token, if the game asks for notifications and you allow them.

Multiplayer: Duel, Tag Team and Party Race

When you play live with other players, the server receives your moves to run the match fairly, and keeps a match record: the mode, the level, each player's account identifier, placing, progress, finish time, move count and coins won. Your Duel rating and your Tag Team partner and recent teammates are stored on your account.

A recent run of yours on a level may be replayed as a Ghost Duel for another player when no live opponent is available. A ghost is always marked with a 👻 and shows your username.

The game has no text chat. Players interact with preset emotes only.

Leaderboards

Your username and score appear on the Daily Canvas, Duel rating and Tag Team leaderboards. If you are signed in to Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android), the game can also post your Duel rating to their leaderboards, under their own privacy terms.

Username moderation, reports and blocks

When you choose a username, its text (not your account or device identifier) is sent to OpenAI's moderation service to check it for offensive content. OpenAI acts as our service provider for this check. If you report another player, we store the report: who reported whom, the reason, the reported username at that time and any short note you add. Reports are used only to keep the game safe.

Gameplay statistics

The game sends gameplay events to our own server: for example sessions, levels started, won or failed, coins and boosters used, ads shown, purchases (product and price), invites, and multiplayer matches. Each event carries a random per-install identifier, your game account identifier, the app version, the platform (iOS or Android) and the language setting. We use them to balance levels, find bugs and understand which features players enjoy. They are not sold and not used for advertising. Our server does not store your IP address with these events.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. To deliver what you bought and to prevent the same purchase from being used twice, our server checks the purchase with Apple's signed receipt data or Google's purchase verification service, and stores the product, the time, the platform and a scrambled (hashed) form of the purchase identifier.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, it may send a crash report to our crash reporting provider, Sentry: technical details such as the device model, OS version, app version and the error. Crash reports do not include your username.

4. Ads, consent and install attribution

  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), Google's consent form asks you first. On iPhone the game asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). AdMob may collect your advertising identifier, your IP address (which can show your approximate location), ad interactions and diagnostics, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • To measure which ad campaigns led to an install, the game sends one install report to our attribution partner NUVIAD (notify.nuviad.com): the random per-install identifier, the app version, the device type and model, OS version, screen size, language and time zone. On iPhone it is sent only after you allow tracking, and then includes the advertising identifier (IDFA). On Android it does not include the advertising identifier. Where the GDPR applies (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) it is not sent at all; elsewhere it is sent only after Google's consent check confirms that no consent is needed where you are.
  • Apple's SKAdNetwork reports installs and a few in-game milestones (for example finishing the tutorial or a first purchase) to ad networks without any identifier.
  • Invite links are never used for ads or attribution.

5. Invite links

When a friend shares an invite or challenge link with you and you open it:

  • The page asks our server for your friend's username and avatar to show who invited you.
  • On an iPhone or iPad only, our server also notes the invite for 2 hours: your friend's code, the time, the word "iOS", and a scrambled (salted hash) form of the first part of your network address (for IPv4 the first three of four numbers, for IPv6 the first three of eight groups). Your full IP address is not stored.
  • If you install the game within those 2 hours, it asks: "Were you invited by {name}?". Only if you answer Yes do you become friends and receive your welcome coins. A matched note is used once, and every note is deleted after 2 hours whether it was used or not.
  • On Android, the invite code travels with the Google Play install (the Play install referrer), so nothing is noted on our server when you open the page.
  • Nothing is read from your clipboard, and invite data is never used for ads, tracking or attribution.

6. How we use information

  • To run the game: your account, friends, challenges, live matches, leaderboards, invites and rewards, and delivering purchases.
  • To keep players safe: username moderation, reports and blocks, and preventing cheating and purchase or invite abuse.
  • To improve the game: gameplay statistics and crash reports.
  • To fund the game: ads, and measuring which campaigns lead to installs.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of our contract with you (running the game and your account), our legitimate interests (safety, anti-cheat, gameplay statistics, crash reports, non-personalised ads) and your consent (personalised ads and tracking, which you can withdraw at any time).

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Who we share it with

  • Other players: your username, avatar, frame, statistics, friend code, leaderboard scores, ghost runs and match results are visible to players you play with or connect to. Your username and avatar appear on your invite links.
  • Google AdMob: ads (see section 4).
  • NUVIAD: the install report described in section 4.
  • OpenAI: username text, for moderation only.
  • Sentry: crash reports.
  • Apple and Google: app store services, purchases and their verification, optional sign-in, Game Center / Play Games leaderboards and SKAdNetwork.
  • Hosting: our game and statistics servers run on a server rented by TonTech from a hosting provider.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

8. Your choices

  • Tracking: decline the tracking prompt, or change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Ad consent (EEA/UK): change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy options.
  • Notifications: turn them off in your device settings.
  • Invites: answer No to "Were you invited by...?" and nothing is linked.
  • Players: block or report anyone from the Friends screen.

9. Deleting your account and data

In the game, open Settings → Delete account and confirm. This permanently deletes, from our game server:

  • your account and its device and sign-in links, your username and friend code;
  • your profile, statistics, Duel rating, Tag Team data, coins, boosters and items;
  • your friends and blocks, challenges, invites and invite rewards, and pending invite notes for your links;
  • your leaderboard entries and ghost runs;
  • your push token and in-game notifications.

Some records are kept without your account: match records (the account identifier in them no longer leads to any profile), reports you made or that were made about you (for safety, see section 10), and purchase verification records, from which your account identifier is removed. Gameplay statistics linked to your account, and those sent from the same install before you signed in, are deleted from our statistics server too, usually within an hour.

Your solo progress is stored on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game. If you can no longer open the game, you can ask us to delete your account by email; see how to delete your account.

10. How long we keep information

  • Account data: until you delete your account, or until we close the game's online services.
  • Challenges: they expire after 72 hours and are deleted within 7 days.
  • Invite notes from iPhone link visits: 2 hours at most. Unanswered invite offers: 7 days.
  • Match records: 12 months.
  • Reports: 12 months after the report was last updated.
  • Gameplay statistics: 24 months.
  • Purchase verification records: as long as needed to prevent the same purchase being used twice and to meet tax and accounting rules.
  • Server logs (which include IP addresses): 30 days.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.

11. Children

Mix & Reveal is a game for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA or UK under the GDPR, or in California under the CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your information, to object to or restrict some uses, and to withdraw consent. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights; we will answer within 30 days. Your information may be processed outside your country, for example by our service providers in the United States, with appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email info@tontech.co. Include your friend code (Settings or the Friends screen) so we can find your account.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted here with a new effective date; important changes are also shown in the game.
